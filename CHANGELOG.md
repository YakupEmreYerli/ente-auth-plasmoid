# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Ente Auth codes in the Plasma panel: search, click to copy, countdowns, service logos.
- Stays in sync with Ente through the official Ente CLI; first-run guide and a setup wizard that signs the CLI in.
- Codes stay encrypted on disk and are unlocked into a small background process by typing a passphrase in the
  widget; secrets are wiped on lock and kept out of swap.
- Codes are handed only to the widget and to interactive terminals.
- English and Turkish.
